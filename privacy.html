Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Dealt does not collect any personal data.

What stays on your device

Your games, times, steps and stats are stored only on your iPhone, by the app itself. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

Dealt has no sign-in, shows no ads, includes no analytics or third-party SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Sharing

When you choose to share a daily result, the iOS share sheet sends the text and image you see to the app you pick. Dealt does not receive a copy.

Children

Dealt is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example, online play), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the App Store privacy details will change with it.
