Common questions

Is every hand solvable? Yes. Each deal is checked by a solver before you see it, and Give Up shows the shortest solution.

Can I leave a daily puzzle and come back? Yes. Your hand and your time are saved. A daily ends only when you solve it or give up.

Where are my stats stored? Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.
